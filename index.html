Mission

Start from first principles. Ask what people truly need, not what AI can show off. Then build it, ship it, and make it better every single week. If it makes the world better, it's worth building — even when the odds say no.

How we think

Engineered for the real world.

Three rules behind every Nakilab product, from the first sketch to the App Store.

01

First principles

We break a problem down to what is actually true, then rebuild the answer from there — not from what everyone else does.

02

Ship, then iterate

A working product in real hands teaches more than any plan. We launch early and improve relentlessly.

03

Useful over impressive

The best technology disappears into everyday life. If it doesn't help a real person, we don't ship it.
